Evolution Story

Why We Split spec-repo Into Four Repositories

In the beginning, spec-repo was a single repository housing simple HTML tag definitions. But as our compiler engine grew, everything became instruction-driven:

  • The Friction: Tracing a component like table.bodyOnly required hopping through 4 completely different sections in one massive repository—hunting for what the base produced, what the JSONPath was querying, and what attributes were legal.
  • The Breakthrough: Recognizing that these are four fundamentally different layers of engineering: Grammar (Tags) → Blueprints (Spec) → Assembly (Instructions) → Contracts (Definitions).
  • The Orchestrator Pattern: Splitting each tier into its own dedicated repository gives every layer 100% single-responsibility focus, while spec-repo orchestrates them for the outside world with zero breaking changes.
